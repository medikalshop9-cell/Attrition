# AI Ethics in Employee Attrition Prediction

## Key Concerns

### 1. Bias
- Historical HR data may reflect discrimination
- Models may reinforce unfair patterns

### 2. Fairness
- Ensure no group is unfairly targeted
- Evaluate predictions across demographics

### 3. Transparency
- Use interpretable models (logistic regression, decision trees)
- Explain results clearly to stakeholders

### 4. Privacy
- Protect employee data
- Avoid misuse of sensitive attributes

## Responsible Use
- Use predictions to support employees, not penalize them
- Focus on improving workplace conditions

## Guiding Principle
AI should enhance human decision-making, not replace it.
